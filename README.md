- 🔭 I am a Professor of Software Engineering at the University of Groningen, the Netherlands
- 🌱 My research interests lie in the area of software architecture, with strong emphasis on architecture modeling, knowledge, evolution, analytics and technical debt
- 👯 I lead the Software Engineering and Architecture research group aka [SEARCH](https://search-rug.github.io/)
- ✒ I am the Editor in Chief of the Journal of Systems and Software ([JSS](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-systems-and-software))
- 🏛+🏭 I champion the evidence-based paradigm in Software Engineering research and works towards closing the gap between industry and academia
